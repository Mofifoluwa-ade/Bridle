# Bridle Brand Guidelines

Oct 23, 2026 · @Mofifoluwa Adelowo

## Brand essence

Bridle is the brand of steering: it makes agent autonomy safe by putting a clear, checkable limit around what an agent can do.

**The line:** Steer your agents, don't trust them.

**What it is:** a NEAR contract that lets an AI agent spend across chains without holding a key, inside a mandate the owner signs once.

**Who it is for:** developers and teams who want agents to move money and need to cap the downside, and anyone who must grasp the idea in one glance, such as a hackathon judge.

**The promise:** you set the limits once, the contract enforces them, and you keep the veto.

**Why "Bridle":** a leash only restrains, but a bridle steers. That matches the thesis that humans are the steering wheel and the agent is the engine. It was also chosen over "Leash" because an established LEASH token already exists.

**Personality**

| Trait | What it means | How it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calm | Reads as something under control, never alarmed | Generous space, one accent color, alert colors only when something needs attention |
| Exact | Names the number, the rule and the limit | Stablecoin amounts, plain-language rules, monospace type for values |
| Candid | States its limits openly | An "honest edges" section, no overclaiming, the simulated-interface label on concept material |

**What the brand is not:** hype, fear, or cute. It avoids drain-and-rug language, horse clipart and anything that sounds like a promise of safety it cannot keep.

## Voice and tone

Bridle sounds like a precise, unhurried colleague: it states the rule, names the limit and never raises its voice.

**Four principles**

1. **Say the rule, not the feeling.** "Up to $50 a day" beats "stay safe".
2. **Plain over clever.** Short sentences, under about 20 words. Favor the verbs that match the product: set, sign, propose, veto, revoke.
3. **Be candid about limits.** If version one does not do something, say so. "Transfers only" is a feature of honesty.
4. **Stay calm under attack.** When something is blocked, say what and why. No exclamation marks and no alarm words.

**Before and after**

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Military-grade AI guard keeps your funds 100% safe | Up to $50 a day, to three approved recipients. |
| Transaction rejected! | Blocked. Recipient not on the list. |
| Trustless agent wallet | An agent wallet with no key to steal. |
| No data yet | Waiting for the agent. |
| Your agent was hacked | The agent tried to overspend. Blocked. |

**Words**

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| mandate, propose, veto, revoke, limit, bond, approved recipient, steer | trustless, unhackable, guaranteed, 100% safe |
| agent, contract, payment, transfer | revolutionary, AI-powered, next-gen, seamless |
| blocked, waiting, frozen | drained, rugged, exploited, nuked |

Bridle does not remove trust in the signing network, so any claim of "trustless" would contradict the product's own honest-edges page.

**Tone by context**

| Where | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Landing page | Confident and spare | Steer your agents, don't trust them. |
| Product interface | Neutral and exact | Waiting for your veto. Above the $20 threshold. |
| Blocked or frozen states | Calm and specific | Agent frozen. Every pending payment is cancelled. |
| Social posts | Plain, one idea per post | Your AI agent doesn't need your private key. It needs a bridle. |
| Docs and README | Candid | v1 limits: transfers only, stablecoin caps, one trust assumption. |

**Mechanics:** sentence case everywhere, including buttons and headings. "Bridle" is always capitalized. Rules use whole dollars ("$50 a day"), and feed amounts show two decimals ($18.00).
